# 2026.331.0121 — feat(repo): the whisper image and its two check beds

## Summary

Move the `whisper` image and its two check beds out of `distro-cachyos` into `pod-whisper`, the repo
that owns the service (`AGENTS.md` "Repo classes and naming": a bed lives with the thing it tests; a
`distro-*` repo holds the distro family, not one service's box).

The box itself is unchanged where it matters — same `layer-whisper` candy pin, same host-bind model
store — and gains the three things a **standalone consumer** must declare, none of which a copy
inherits:

1. **The base as a namespaced import.** In `distro-cachyos` the box said `base: cachyos`, a LOCAL
   name. Here it is `base: cachyos.cachyos`, with
   `import: [- cachyos: '@github.com/opencharly/distro-cachyos:v2026.330.1852']` at the **project
   root** (a namespace mounted by a discovered document does not resolve the box's own `base:`).
2. **The pixi builder vocabulary.** `defaults.builder.pixi: arch.arch-builder`, with
   `- arch: '@github.com/opencharly/distro-arch:v2026.330.1808'` imported — without it the two pixi
   candies fail validation with "no builder.pixi configured".
3. **`layer-supervisord` pinned in the box.** The base declares `init: supervisord`; its
   `depends_candy` is materialized only when a candy in the SCANNED set names it, so a standalone
   consumer pins it or the image declares an init with no implementation.
